# Gridium cross-study consistency audit

The scientific picture is coherent **when each result keeps its model, basis, control family and metric attached**. There is good evidence for an internally checked implementation, strong low-energy couplings, finite-model logical controllability and a collective shelving mechanism. There is no validated realistic four-mode X gate, no convergence certificate beyond k180, and no device-specific prediction for the missing array mode.

The important corrections are to scope and interpretation, rather than a reversal of the central numerical results. The [48-claim ledger](claim_ledger.csv) assigns one primary classification per claim, with exact artifact/line or data-selector citations, assumptions and falsification conditions. Established statements there concern the specified stored matrices, not the physical circuit. [24 contradictions and qualifications](contradictions.csv) distinguish real inconsistencies, different questions, convention mismatches and overinterpretations.

## Findings that change how the results should be used

1. **“0721 is most robust” needs an objective.** It is an informative retained-mask and state-8-avoidance comparator. It is not a universal winner: saved N51→N71 path-product changes are −2.951% for 0781, +1.607% for 0721 and −0.0748% for 0381. The original pathway audit deliberately uses a Pareto comparison, not a weighted robustness score. Conversely, the older adverse N71 masks favor 0721. Keep both comparators. See claims C09–C11 and contradictions X01–X02.

2. **“More extended states” needs a coordinate.** For states 0/1, theta2 standard deviations are 2.643/2.659; for 7/8 they are smaller, 2.507/2.482. Theta3 widths increase from 2.751/2.757 to 3.453/3.261. Stage1–mode4 entropy increases from about 0.625/0.628 to 1.026/1.021 bits. Greater mixing and theta3 extension are supported; broadening in every direction is not. Small saved cutoff tails establish neither absolute convergence nor a bound on operator error. [Characterization data](../state_0178_checkpoint_diagnostics.json), C04–C06.

3. **The 70 ns result is an axis-calibratable mechanism example, not a fixed-X validation.** Recomputing the metric from the saved two logical columns reproduces best-equatorial-pi overlap **0.9810509903**, final mean leakage **0.0160568843**, and an axis angle **1.54630697 rad** in the saved bare-drift interaction frame. Against X in that same frame, the overlap is **0.3283726637**. This is not a newly discovered pulse failure: the original report correctly allows the axis to vary. It shows why the shorthand “98.1% X” would be wrong. A calibrated axis/frame correction needs a declared target and feasible implementation; laboratory-frame performance is not determined here. [Saved columns](../driven_selectivity_n71/final_propagators.npz), [metric arithmetic](arithmetic_checks.json), C17–C20.

4. **Final and transient leakage answer different questions.** The saved sampled peak **mean** leakage is 0.992689, despite the 0.016057 final mean. Shelving intentionally leaves the bare logical subspace and returns most population. This is not irreversible loss in a closed model; it also does not demonstrate protection against dissipation or noise. The figure is neither a worst-input leakage bound nor a rigorously located continuous-time maximum. The 32→48-state overlap change is about 0.001460, far larger than the 7.4e−10 integration-tolerance change. [Pulse table](../driven_selectivity_n71/final_checks.csv), X05–X07.

5. **The raw path product is not an effective coupling or gate ranking.** At the declared weak-drive comparison point, the primary/comparator full third-order coupling ratio is about 4.55, versus 11.92 for raw products. The 500 ns prescribed pulse comparison favors 0721 over 0781, whereas fast shelving favors a different mechanism. Neither is a globally optimized comparison under common amplitude/bandwidth constraints. Signed loop phases, all relevant denominators, photon orderings, Stark shifts and occupied-state leakage matter. [Driven report](../driven_selectivity_n71/REPORT.md), C07–C08, C16.

6. **Restricted control does not always mean a smaller reachable algebra.** The tested high-only first-order RWA separates the logical sectors; the tested two-band RWA with residual drift and independent signed envelopes still generates su(4). Neither result settles an unspecified hard bandwidth or a fixed finite-parameter pulse family. The unrestricted result applies to tested projections through **48 states**, not automatically to the entire 1440-dimensional parent or infinite circuit. The independent audit's graph/frequency certificate is stronger than the near-tolerance 48-state numerical closure. [Independent audit](../controllability_n71/audit/AUDIT_REPORT.md), C21–C27. Its sufficient criterion agrees with [Altafini, Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0110147).

7. **Convergence evidence shares an endpoint and is not independent physical confirmation.** Characterization, pathway, susceptibility, driven and controllability studies inherit the same circuit assumptions and checkpoint; several reuse the same signed reduction. Agreement among them validates internal consistency and some independent algorithms. Oracle-informed retained masks were selected against k180 and then scored against k180. Their agreement is useful engineering evidence, but not an out-of-parent test. k220 is the intended next such test. [Selection method](../n71_retained_subspace_postprocess.json), [re-audit](../k_convergence_reaudit/REPORT.md), C28–C37, C45.

8. **Array risk is plausible, not measured for this benchmark.** The [paper, Supplementary Notes 6 and 11](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14656v1), gives a distributed-array model and device-specific 4.83 GHz, 5.5–6.5 GHz and above-8 GHz examples. These do not fix the benchmark's mode or coupling. The implemented 15.845 GHz bare Cp node oscillator contains no internal island-chain coordinate. Effective fitted parameters could nevertheless absorb some omitted-mode effects, making double counting a real concern. A predictive extension needs branch identity, normalized participation and consistent capacitance/stiffness reduction. [Array audit](../array_mode_extension_audit.md), C39–C42. A near-resonant mode may reorganize all three paths; it does not necessarily destroy any of them.

## Actual documentation inconsistencies and overclaims

These are recorded without editing their sources:

- `EmilianoUPDATE.md`, the paragraph beginning “Successfully validated … N=71, k=180”, says the main issue is pulse control rather than spatial convergence. That **overstates** the evidence. N51→N71 moves individual low-11 excitations by up to 40.39 MHz and the selectivity mismatch from approximately 9.029 to 6.876 MHz. Clean identity tracking does not settle spatial accuracy. This historical summary must not override the later quantified limitations.
- `docs/RESULTS_LEDGER.md` still says no four-mode asymmetric model is implemented. It is a **stale status statement**, not a description of the current source tree.
- The original controllability report links a missing root-level `workspace_verification.json`. Its independent audit supplies a valid preservation artifact. This is a documentation defect, not a failed controllability result.
- The synthetic tracking report's “all four individual overlap probabilities 0.5” describes four entries of a **two-state 2×2** overlap matrix. There are two ambiguous central states; the exterior states remain unchanged. This wording defect does not invalidate the synthetic test.
- The array report's instruction to detect individual ambiguity “when singular values show ambiguity” conflates two diagnostics. Row/column competition diagnoses label ambiguity; singular values diagnose capture/continuity of a whole subspace. An identically preserved span can have ambiguous individual labels.

Exact source locations are X11–X14 and X18–X20 in [contradictions.csv](contradictions.csv). The old implementation specification is explicitly a staged/source-boundary document: its unresolved combined-model question remains relevant even though a netlist implementation now exists. Do not merge equations from its S23 gauge with the present netlist gauge without a derivation.

## Convention audit

| Convention | Verified usage and required qualification |
|---|---|
| Energy/state ordering | Labels are zero-based final eigenstate ranks at the reference. Retained cases must map through overlaps; ambiguous clusters do not acquire unique physical descendants from a forced assignment. |
| Frequencies/units | Energies H/h and drive coefficients are GHz; ordinary-frequency differences multiply by 1000 for MHz; time is ns. Driven evolution uses −i2πh. No GHz/MHz or 2π mismatch was found in the inspected driven formulas/code. |
| Rabi convention | For A cos(2πνt)Φ, the isolated RWA off-diagonal is AΦ/2, cyclic population Rabi frequency A|Φ|, rectangular pi time 1/(2A|Φ|). A pi pulse is not time 1/Ω in this convention. |
| Signed vs absolute Φ | Static ranking uses magnitudes. Driven/Lie studies use signed matrices; the 0178 loop product is negative in a gauge-invariant sense. Replacing Φ by |Φ| would change interference. Independent eigenvector phases may change individual signs without changing physics. |
| Path product | Product of three absolute edges over 72 simple low11 paths. “Rank 1” does not cover every higher-state path, resolvent, waveform or physical port. |
| State overlap | Earlier saved `state_overlaps` are amplitudes. New tracker P is their square. State-8 spatial amplitude ≈0.99906748 corresponds to P≈0.99813583. |
| Ambiguity | The amplitude margin is √Passigned−√Pcompetitor; it is not the squared probability margin. Warning gates differ from convergence tolerances. |
| Subspace/operator comparison | Whiten nonorthogonal spatial frames before principal angles. Align both endpoints of rectangular blocks. The 22.124% and 24.550% 45/46 changes differ only by denominator, not data. The internal near-zero block is not that outgoing block. |
| N51/N71 | N2=N3 grid sizes, with actual domains/cutoffs recorded separately. This is numerical spatial refinement, not a change of device physics. |
| k and parent semantics | k is retained nonlinear-sector dimension; explicit masks/families matter. Stage2 dimension is k×N4. 48 is a driven/eigenstate window, not k. Frozen k180/200/220 must share the new k220 parent; the old k180 checkpoint is not automatically that nested k180 case. |
| Gate overlap | F=[Tr(K†K)+|Tr(V†K)|²]/6 averages over logical inputs and includes leakage; V/frame/axis must be stated. It is not process fidelity, postselected fidelity, a worst-case error or a mere transfer probability. |
| Leakage | Final mean outside the bare logical subspace differs from sampled peak mean and from irreversible physical loss. Both input columns are needed. |
| Control operator | grid_phi=theta3+theta2/2; bias derivatives have GHz/radian units and different physical roles. u is an abstract coefficient, not a calibrated line voltage/flux. |
| Parameter variations | +1% energy and +0.01 asymmetry are different normalization conventions. Both are local mathematical scenarios, not fitted error bars. S33 uses angular ω; its GHz frequency examples must be interpreted as ω/(2π). |
| Error directions | State-tracking displacement in the re-audit is endpoint minus case excitation; low-energy/path tables use case minus endpoint. Relative edge errors use the endpoint, whereas the original pair-block percentage uses k170. |

The frozen k220 criteria classify magnitudes and scalar overlap amplitudes. They do not by themselves certify signed-loop stability, full cluster operators or driven gates. Request signed matrices and vector/overlap sidecars if available, preserving frozen criteria and labeling any additional acceptance rules separately.

## Audit verification and limits

`audit.py` performs only matrix-coefficient arithmetic, saved-array comparisons, gate-metric recomputation and artifact/definition checks. It does not import or execute reconstruction/propagation/closure drivers. **15 checks pass**, including the energy-cycle identity, signed loop, cross-study matrix consistency, saved gate metric, three path products, coefficient matrices, amplitude/probability conversions, synthetic matrix scope, frozen parent contract and missing-link diagnosis. See [arithmetic_checks.json](arithmetic_checks.json).

This is not a rerun of every historical test or an independent physical derivation. Primary-source checks were limited to the array discussion and controllability sufficient theorem; no new source equation was adopted. Remote job status and Thomas's current availability were not checked or inferred. [verification.json](verification.json) records document/ledger consistency and preservation of all 465 pre-existing tracked, sandbox and checkpoint files captured at start. No original report or production file was edited; no eigensolve, pulse optimization, commit or push occurred.
